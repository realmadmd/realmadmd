## Who I Am

I'm **Daud Ahmed** — a **Full Stack Architecture Developer** focused on designing and shipping scalable digital products for startups, businesses, and enterprises.

I don't just write features. I design **end-to-end software ecosystems** — from multi-tenant SaaS platforms and healthcare systems to AI-powered workflows, enterprise dashboards, mobile applications, and automation platforms.

My work sits at the intersection of product thinking and system design. I care about what users need to accomplish, how teams operate day to day, and how the underlying architecture will behave when traffic, roles, and business rules expand.

I believe software should:

- solve real operational problems instead of adding more manual process
- reduce repetitive work across teams and departments
- create measurable business growth through clearer workflows
- keep performing when users, data, and complexity increase

## What I Build

I build complete product systems — not isolated screens, scripts, or disconnected modules. Each product is designed as a coherent ecosystem: frontend, backend, data, access control, and operational workflows working together.

### SaaS Platforms

Multi-tenant applications built for scale. I design subscription foundations, onboarding flows, organization structures, role systems, billing-ready architecture, and modular feature growth so products can expand across customers without becoming fragile.

### Enterprise Software

Operational systems, ERP-style platforms, internal business tools, approval flows, RFQ platforms, vendor portals, and workflow automation. The goal is simple: help teams move faster with fewer bottlenecks and clearer accountability.

### Healthcare Platforms

Appointment management, patient records, doctor dashboards, clinic administration, digital prescriptions, lab booking, billing, and multi-role healthcare operations. These systems need reliability, clear roles, and workflows that match how clinics actually run.

### Mobile Applications

iOS and Android applications using modern frameworks and scalable architectures. Mobile clients are treated as first-class product surfaces backed by clean APIs, auth, and maintainable data contracts.

### AI Solutions

AI agents, intelligent business systems, workflow automation, and custom integrations with OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, and n8n. I use AI where it removes repetitive work and strengthens decision support — not as decoration.

### UI/UX, 3D & Product Animation

Modern user experiences focused on conversion, usability, and scalability. This includes design systems, interactive product visualization, 3D web experiences, and product animations that communicate value clearly.

### 01 — DoctorSpace

**Healthcare management platform for modern clinics**

#### Overview

DoctorSpace is a comprehensive healthcare platform built for clinics and patients. It combines appointment booking, doctor discovery, digital prescriptions, lab tests, patient engagement tools, and multi-role clinic operations into one production-ready product experience.

#### Problem Solved

Clinics often run on fragmented tools — phone bookings, paper records, disconnected billing, and no patient portal. That creates delays, poor visibility, and repeated manual work for staff and doctors. DoctorSpace unifies those workflows into a single digital system designed around real clinic operations.

#### Overview

FVTURE is the digital product layer for Bangkok’s FVTURE hyperclub — a high-capacity nightlife venue experience covering event discovery, ticket booking, VIP table reservations, content, and companion mobile access. The product is designed to match the premium quality of the physical venue.

#### Problem Solved

Premium nightlife needs more than a static website. Guests need fast ticket purchase, VIP booking, event updates, and a branded experience that feels as polished as the venue itself. FVTURE brings content, commerce, and event operations into one digital ecosystem.
