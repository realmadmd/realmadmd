I'm **Daud Ahmed** — a **Full Stack Architecture Developer** focused on designing and shipping scalable digital products for startups, businesses, and enterprises.

I don't just write features. I design **end-to-end software ecosystems** — from multi-tenant SaaS platforms and healthcare systems to AI-powered workflows, enterprise dashboards, mobile applications, and automation platforms.

My work sits at the intersection of product thinking and system design. I care about what users need to accomplish, how teams operate day to day, and how the underlying architecture will behave when traffic, roles, and business rules expand.

I believe software should:

## 01 — DoctorSpace

<div align="center">

### Healthcare management platform for modern clinics

<a href="https://doctorspace.pk"><img src="https://image.thum.io/get/width/1200/crop/560/noanimate/https://doctorspace.pk" alt="DoctorSpace preview" width="100%" /></a>

<br /><br />

<a href="https://doctorspace.pk"><img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-doctorspace.pk-34D399?style=for-the-badge&logo=googlechrome&logoColor=white" alt="Live" /></a>

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Healthcare-SaaS-0F172A?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=020617&color=34D399" alt="Category" />

</div>

<br />

**Overview**  
DoctorSpace is a comprehensive healthcare platform built for clinics and patients. It combines appointment booking, doctor discovery, digital prescriptions, lab tests, patient engagement tools, and multi-role clinic operations into one production-ready product experience.

**Problem Solved**  
Clinics often run on fragmented tools — phone bookings, paper records, disconnected billing, and no patient portal. That creates delays, poor visibility, and repeated manual work. DoctorSpace unifies those workflows into a single digital system designed around real clinic operations.

**Product Highlights**

- **Clinic Ops** — doctor management, staff management, multi-role access, clinic administration  
- **Patient Care** — patient records, appointments, digital prescriptions, care continuity  
- **Diagnostics** — lab test booking for home collection or in-clinic visits  
- **Discovery** — specialty browsing across dental, gynae, physio, nutrition, and more  
- **Engagement** — health calculators, blogs, patient portal experiences  
- **Business** — billing, reporting, and operational visibility for clinic leadership  

**Architecture Notes**

- Multi-role access model for doctors, staff, and patients  
- Appointment and prescription workflows designed for real clinic operations  
- Mobile-friendly patient journeys with secure health data handling  
- Product structure built for ongoing healthcare operations, not just marketing pages  
- Clear separation between patient-facing journeys and internal clinic administration  

**Stack:** `Next.js` · `NestJS` · `PostgreSQL` · `TypeScript` · `RBAC`  
**Live:** [doctorspace.pk](https://doctorspace.pk)

## 02 — FVTURE

<div align="center">

### Digital platform for a world-class nightlife venue experience

<a href="https://fvture.co"><img src="https://image.thum.io/get/width/1200/crop/560/noanimate/https://fvture.co" alt="FVTURE preview" width="100%" /></a>

<br />

**Overview**  
FVTURE is the digital product layer for Bangkok’s FVTURE hyperclub — a high-capacity nightlife venue experience covering event discovery, ticket booking, VIP table reservations, content, and companion mobile access. The product is designed to match the premium quality of the physical venue.

**Problem Solved**  
Premium nightlife needs more than a static website. Guests need fast ticket purchase, VIP booking, event updates, and a branded experience that feels as polished as the venue itself. FVTURE brings content, commerce, and event operations into one digital ecosystem.

**Product Highlights**

- **Events** — upcoming schedules, artist / DJ discovery, event-driven product surfaces  
- **Ticketing** — entry ticket booking with a direct digital purchase flow  
- **VIP** — table reservations and premium hospitality experiences  
- **Content** — blog and editorial surfaces for venue storytelling and updates  
- **Mobile** — companion app for tickets, VIP booking, and live event updates  
- **Experience** — immersive brand-led UX aligned with a premium nightlife product  

**Architecture Notes**

- Consumer booking and reservation flows designed for event-driven demand  
- Web and mobile surfaces connected to one venue ecosystem  
- High-intent conversion design around book table / book ticket actions  
- Content, commerce, and event operations unified in one product experience  
- Built to support peak-interest traffic around launches and major events  

**Stack:** Modern web stack · APIs · Mobile companion · Cloud deployment  
**Live:** [fvture.co](https://fvture.co)

I work architecture-first and outcome-driven. Before shipping screens, I define the system shape: data, access, boundaries, and growth path. Then I build product surfaces that stay maintainable as the business expands.

**01 · Discover**  
Understand business goals, users, constraints, operational pain points, and success metrics.

**02 · Architect**  
Define tenancy, data model, access control, API boundaries, and module structure.

**03 · Build**  
Ship clean, typed, maintainable product surfaces across web, dashboards, and mobile.

**04 · Automate**  
Add workflows, AI assist, queues, and ops tooling where automation creates real leverage.

**05 · Scale**  
Optimize performance, reliability, infrastructure, and observability as usage grows.

**Principles I ship with**

- **Architecture before features** — structure first so growth does not force expensive rewrites  
- **Business outcomes over vanity UI** — every module should reduce friction or unlock growth  
- **Security by design** — auth, roles, and data boundaries are part of the foundation  
- **Performance as a product feature** — speed and reliability are part of the experience  
- **Ship complete systems** — frontend, backend, data, and operations as one coherent product
